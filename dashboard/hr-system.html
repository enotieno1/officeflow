HR Operating Framework

The essential functions of a complete HR office

A structured view of the people operations, records and controls that keep an organization staffed, supported and compliant.

Core HR priorities
01 · Foundation

Employee Records & HR Administration

Maintain accurate employee profiles, job and department details, contracts, qualifications, next-of-kin details, employment history, personnel files and approved changes.

Establishes the authoritative employee record.

Open Employees
02 · Critical operation

Payroll & Compensation

Manage salary and allowance inputs, overtime, approved deductions, bonuses, statutory obligations, payslips, payroll reviews and final dues in coordination with Finance.

Supports accurate, timely and controlled pay.

Open Payroll
03 · Daily control

Attendance, Time & Leave

Track attendance, lateness, absence, shifts, overtime, leave applications, approvals, balances and return-to-work dates.

Helps plan staffing and verify time-related pay.

Open Attendance
04 · Workforce lifecycle

Recruitment, Onboarding & Separation

Coordinate workforce requests, vacancies, applications, interviews, selection approvals, offers, induction, probation, transfers, offboarding and exit clearance.

Controls how people join, move through and leave the organization.

05 · Protection

Employee Relations & Compliance

Handle grievances, disciplinary cases, employee welfare, workplace disputes, policy acknowledgements, confidentiality and applicable employment-law requirements.

Promotes fair processes and reduces people-related risk.

Open Reports & Compliance
06 · Improvement

Performance, Learning & Workforce Insights

Coordinate goals and reviews, training plans, skills development, succession planning, workforce budgets, headcount reports and HR performance indicators.

Helps management develop people and plan ahead.

Open Reports & Compliance

Supporting HR responsibilities

  • Benefits, pensions and employee claims
  • Contracts, job descriptions and organization structure
  • Health, safety, wellbeing and engagement
  • Promotions, transfers and internal mobility
  • Equal opportunity and inclusion

Daily HR service cycle

  1. Receive a request or record an employee event.
  2. Validate the details and supporting documents.
  3. Route to the authorized reviewer or approver.
  4. Record the decision and complete the action.
  5. Update the employee record and retain an audit trail.
  6. Escalate overdue actions and report exceptions.

Essential management controls

  • Restrict sensitive records to authorized roles.
  • Separate preparation, approval and payment duties.
  • Keep dated approvals and change history.
  • Flag expiring contracts, probation and overdue actions.
  • Protect confidentiality and follow retention rules.

The HR priority order

Reliable employee records → controlled HR administration → attendance and leave → accurate payroll → recruitment and employee lifecycle → compliance → performance and workforce planning.
