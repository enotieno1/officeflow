Finance operations

Finance Office Activity Centre

Core activities for a complete finance department. Expand any area to review its day-to-day responsibilities.

Daily finance routine

Review balances and due dates, register documents, verify requests, post transactions, process authorized payments, reconcile differences and record follow-ups.

Weekly controls

Payment runs, debtor follow-ups, supplier statement checks, cash-flow updates and expenditure reviews.

Monthly close

Payroll, bank and ledger reconciliations, accruals, prepayments, budget variance reports, management accounts and tax deadlines due.

01 · Accounts payable — supplier invoices and payments
  • Register invoices; match invoices to purchase orders, contracts and delivery/service evidence.
  • Validate supplier identity and bank-detail changes; check duplicate invoices, approvals and budget availability.
  • Prepare payment vouchers and authorized payment batches; schedule due dates and record liabilities.
  • Reconcile supplier statements, track overdue bills and retain payment evidence.
02 · Accounts receivable — billing and collections
  • Issue invoices and receipts; allocate incoming payments to the correct customer accounts.
  • Monitor outstanding debts, send reminders, reconcile customer accounts and prepare ageing reports.
  • Process approved credit notes and refunds; escalate overdue or potentially irrecoverable debts.
03 · Cash, bank and treasury operations
  • Monitor bank and authorized cash balances; maintain cashbooks and daily cash-position reports.
  • Perform bank reconciliations; investigate unmatched, duplicate or unexplained entries and bank charges.
  • Manage petty cash and replenishment evidence; forecast cash requirements and plan payment timing.
  • Control bank accounts, signatories and payment instructions under approved mandates.
04 · Budgeting and budgetary control
  • Collect departmental estimates; prepare annual budgets, funding forecasts and cost-centre allocations.
  • Check budget availability before commitments and track actuals against approved budgets.
  • Analyse overspending/underspending, prepare variance explanations and forecast year-end results.
  • Process authorized budget reallocations and supplementary budgets where applicable.
05 · General ledger and accounting
  • Maintain chart of accounts; code and post transactions, journals and approved corrections.
  • Record accruals, prepayments, depreciation and other period-end adjustments.
  • Reconcile subsidiary ledgers and control accounts to the general ledger; investigate suspense balances.
  • Prepare trial balances and close accounting periods with a complete supporting audit trail.
06 · Payroll and staff payments
  • Validate approved staff changes, salaries, allowances, overtime, bonuses and authorized deductions.
  • Calculate net pay and applicable PAYE, NSSF, SHIF and other statutory obligations.
  • Prepare payroll summaries, obtain approval, issue payment instructions and reconcile payroll to the ledger.
  • Remit deductions by applicable deadlines and protect confidential payroll records.
07 · Tax and statutory compliance
  • Maintain a filing and payment calendar for applicable VAT, PAYE, withholding and income tax obligations.
  • Prepare returns, reconcile tax accounts, arrange authorized remittances and retain supporting records.
  • Respond to tax authority queries and track assessments, objections and compliance certificates.
  • Confirm obligations and deadlines according to the entity's legal form and current Kenyan rules.
08 · Financial reporting and management accounts
  • Prepare monthly management accounts, income/expenditure reports, financial position and cash-flow reports.
  • Produce budget performance, departmental spending, revenue and expense trend reports.
  • Explain material variances, monitor financial indicators and prepare executive/board finance papers.
  • Prepare quarterly and annual statements and schedules as required.
09 · Procurement and expenditure control
  • Review requisitions for budget availability and verify authorized procurement documentation.
  • Match purchase orders, contracts, delivery notes and invoices before payment.
  • Track commitments, charge costs to the right budget/cost centre and flag unauthorized spending.
  • Maintain separation between requesting, approving, receiving, recording and paying duties.
10 · Fixed assets and inventory accounting
  • Maintain the fixed-asset register, asset identifiers, locations, custodians and purchase details.
  • Calculate depreciation; verify assets physically and reconcile registers to ledger balances.
  • Record authorized transfers, disposals, impairments and losses.
  • Reconcile stock records to physical counts and investigate shortages or valuation differences.
11 · Internal controls and fraud risk
  • Enforce approval limits and separation of duties; review supporting documents and unusual journals.
  • Flag duplicate invoices/payments, split purchases, unusual spending and supplier bank-detail changes.
  • Document exceptions, preserve audit evidence and escalate concerns to authorized reviewers.
  • Treat alerts as indicators requiring human investigation, not proof of fraud.
12 · Internal and external audit support
  • Prepare audit schedules, ledger extracts, bank reconciliations, vouchers and asset/liability schedules.
  • Answer audit queries with documented evidence and track findings and agreed corrective actions.
  • Monitor outstanding recommendations and support annual financial statement audits.
  • Preserve auditor independence from preparing or authorizing the transactions being audited.
13 · Financial planning and cash-flow forecasting
  • Forecast inflows, outflows, cash shortages, funding needs and major payment dates.
  • Model financial scenarios, analyse operating costs and assess capital expenditure proposals.
  • Monitor debt obligations and financial risks; advise leadership on affordability and savings.
14 · Loans, borrowings and investments
  • Maintain loan schedules, principal/interest calculations, repayments and lender reconciliations.
  • Monitor covenants and maturity dates; record borrowing costs.
  • Where authorized, track approved investments, returns, maturities and performance.
15 · Grants, donor funds and project accounting
  • Record grants and funding restrictions; maintain project budgets and cost allocations.
  • Monitor eligible expenditure against grant terms, reconcile balances and prepare donor reports.
  • Track unspent funds, supporting evidence and project close-out requirements.
16 · Finance administration and document control
  • Register and file invoices, receipts, payment vouchers, bank statements, tax records and agreements.
  • Track pending approvals, missing documents, correspondence and outstanding finance actions.
  • Apply retention rules, confidentiality and role-based access; retrieve records for authorized users.
17 · Finance systems and master data
  • Maintain accounting codes, supplier/customer records, tax codes, cost centres and accounting periods.
  • Control finance-system permissions and review changes to sensitive master data.
  • Validate imports/integrations, investigate posting errors and preserve logs and backups under policy.
18 · Financial risk and insurance
  • Monitor bad-debt, foreign-exchange, interest-rate and contractual exposures where relevant.
  • Track insurance premiums, renewals, claims and the financial impact of losses.
  • Escalate material risks, liabilities and control weaknesses to management.
19 · Cost accounting and cost management
  • Calculate product, service, unit, project and departmental costs.
  • Allocate overheads, compare actual with standard costs and investigate cost variances.
  • Identify inefficiencies and provide cost evidence for pricing and operating decisions.
20 · Year-end financial closure
  • Confirm period cut-off; reconcile banks, receivables, payables, payroll, tax, assets and inventory.
  • Review accruals, prepayments, provisions and suspense accounts; prepare the final trial balance.
  • Prepare annual statements and audit schedules, post approved adjustments, close the period and archive records.

Operational note: this activity centre documents the finance department's scope. A listed responsibility is not automatically an active transaction workflow or statutory filing integration. Payments, payroll, tax submissions, accounting postings and approvals must use authorized, tested processes.

Secure file upload/storage, outbound email delivery, external AI-provider calls, recurring calendar reminders, automatic fraud detection, and billing activation require separate server-side integrations and end-to-end tests.
