# OfficeFlow Brand System
**Version:** November 2026  
**Brand:** OfficeFlow  
**Descriptor:** Africa's Office Operating System  
**Tagline:** Making Operations Simpler

## Positioning
OfficeFlow is one secure workspace for HR, finance, workflows and documents, designed for growing African organisations. The brand should communicate operational clarity, accountability, control and practical usefulness—not inflated enterprise claims.

## Visual identity
- **Primary navy:** #16213E
- **Secondary navy:** #1F2C4F
- **Brass:** #A6823D
- **Light brass:** #C9A868
- **Ink:** #152238
- **Muted text:** #5B6472
- **Typography:** Inter for product UI and body copy; Source Serif 4 may be used sparingly for editorial/investor headlines where available.
- **Logo files:** `/assets/officeflow-logo.svg` (light background) and `/assets/officeflow-logo-inverse.svg` (dark background).

## Logo usage
- Use the supplied SVG assets; do not recreate the wordmark with arbitrary text, emoji or a generic icon.
- Preserve aspect ratio and clear space. Do not stretch, recolour, rotate, add effects or place the inverse logo on a light background.
- Use the light logo on white/light surfaces and inverse logo on navy/dark surfaces.
- Minimum recommended displayed width: 150 px for the full wordmark. Use an approved standalone mark only where the layout cannot accommodate the wordmark.
- Provide meaningful alt text: "OfficeFlow".
- Apply consistently to public pages, authentication, dashboard navigation, department portals, printable exports, slide deck and social templates.

## Brand voice
- Professional, clear, confident and grounded.
- Lead with the workflow outcome: ownership, routing, approvals, traceability and visibility.
- Use plain language. Explain technical terms only when they help the buyer.
- Describe current capability precisely. Distinguish implemented, tested, configured and planned functionality.

## Approved core message
"OfficeFlow brings HR, finance, workflows and documents into one secure workspace—helping growing organisations structure everyday operations and keep work accountable."

## Approved product narrative
"Request → Route → Review / Approve → Execute → Audit."

## Claims policy
Do not publish claims such as guaranteed time savings, customer ROI, customer counts, revenue, "military-grade" or "bank-level" security, certifications, 24/7 support, statutory payroll compliance, offline-first capability, live AI, or third-party integrations unless current evidence supports the exact claim and its scope.

Avoid the previous draft's unsupported phrases such as "save 50% of admin time", "100% compliance", "DEFCON-compliant", "quantum-resistant", "24/7 dedicated support", and "40–60% time savings". These are not approved marketing claims.

## Photography and graphics
Use authentic, restrained workplace imagery and real product captures from a clean test organisation. Avoid fabricated testimonials, customer logos, fake dashboards, synthetic KPI screenshots or decorative "AI" visuals that imply unbuilt functionality.

## Channel adaptations
- Website: product outcome, clear feature scope, honest trial/pricing terms and a demo request.
- Investor materials: problem, insight, product wedge, stage, validation plan, founder ambition and explicit ask.
- Social: short operational pain points and verified product journeys.
- Product UI: clarity and consistency over marketing language.
