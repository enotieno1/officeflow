# OfficeFlow Market Readiness Pack
**Prepared:** 25 November 2026  
**Product:** OfficeFlow — Africa's Office Operating System  
**Positioning:** One secure workspace for HR, finance, workflows and documents, designed for growing African organisations.  
**Readiness stance:** Working MVP / early-stage B2B SaaS moving into customer validation. This pack is not evidence of production readiness, customer traction, revenue, certifications or completed integrations.

## 1. Executive pitch

OfficeFlow is a multi-tenant operations platform designed to help African organisations move fragmented internal work out of disconnected spreadsheets, email threads, paper files and messaging conversations. It brings structured requests, routing, approvals, tasks, documents and audit history into one workspace.

The initial wedge is operational coordination: a request is submitted, routed to the right person or department, reviewed and approved, executed, and retained with an audit trail. The product is intended for growing organisations that need stronger visibility and accountability without assembling a patchwork of separate tools.

**Stage:** Working MVP / early-stage B2B SaaS; moving into structured customer validation and controlled pilots.  
**Not claimed:** paying customers, revenue, quantified customer savings, full production readiness, statutory payroll compliance, independent security certification, or live third-party integrations unless separately evidenced.

## 2. Founder and problem narrative for Antler

Across organisations, everyday work is often split across email, spreadsheets, paper forms and messaging applications. A request can be submitted in one channel, discussed in another, approved verbally, and later become difficult to trace. Managers struggle to see ownership, status, delays and accountability across departments.

OfficeFlow is being built around a simple operating sequence:

**Request → Route → Review / Approve → Execute → Audit**

The founder's motivation is to build an operating layer that makes everyday office work structured and visible. The long-term ambition is to build Africa's leading business operating system and then compete globally. The near-term focus is deliberately narrower: validate the operational workflow wedge with real organisations, learn which workflows create repeat usage, and earn the right to expand.

## 5. Per-module video plan

Each clip should be 30–45 seconds, show one real user outcome, and end with the same OfficeFlow closing frame. Do not show placeholder data or imply integrations are live.

1. **HR Management:** employee record lifecycle and role-restricted access. Only show workflows that have passed create/edit/view and permission tests.
2. **Finance & Administration:** submit and review a finance/administrative request; show approval controls. Do not present as a full accounting package or statutory payroll engine.
3. **Tasks & Workflow:** create a request, route it, approve/reject it and show the resulting status. This is the priority demo wedge.
4. **Document Management:** upload a real test file to private storage, retrieve it with authorized access, and show metadata/audit. Do not use a record-only placeholder.
5. **Email & Communication:** show in-app notifications only if verified. Show outbound email only after provider delivery, bounce and retry tests.
6. **Calendar & Scheduling:** currently treat as not demo-ready until persistent event storage and create/edit/list flows are verified.
7. **AI Productivity:** do not publish a feature video until a real provider response, permission-aware retrieval, source grounding and human-review controls pass tests.
8. **Reports & Insights:** show only live metrics reconciled to source records and filtered to the signed-in user's permitted scope.
9. **Security & Access:** demonstrate role and tenant isolation with negative tests; do not claim certification or "military-grade" security.
