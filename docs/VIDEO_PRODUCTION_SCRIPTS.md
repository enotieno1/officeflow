# OfficeFlow Video Production Scripts
**Version:** 25 November 2026  
**Production rule:** record only real product flows in a clean test organisation. If a workflow is not verified, do not stage a fake success state. Mark the clip "in development" or omit it.

## Master demo — 2:45
**Opening (0:00–0:15)**  
Visual: simple, clean typography showing email / spreadsheet / paper fragments.  
Voiceover: "Important office work is still spread across disconnected tools. Requests, approvals and supporting records can become difficult to follow."

**Product introduction (0:15–0:35)**  
Visual: public homepage, then sign in to a test organisation.  
Voiceover: "OfficeFlow is building one workspace for HR, finance, workflows and documents, designed for growing African organisations."

**Core workflow (0:35–1:35)**  
Visual: create a test request, assign/rout it, sign in as an authorised approver, approve or reject, inspect the resulting state. Capture the actual workflow and audit records.  
Voiceover: "A process begins with a request, moves to the responsible person, and records the decision. Ownership and status stay connected to the work."

**Execution and records (1:35–2:05)**  
Visual: show a related task or document only if the file and task journeys are fully verified.  
Voiceover: "The aim is to keep execution and supporting records connected to the original process."

**Visibility (2:05–2:25)**  
Visual: role-authorised dashboard/report and matching source record.  
Voiceover: "Teams can review the work they are authorised to see and trace activity through its history."

**Close (2:25–2:45)**  
Visual: logo, product name, website and demo invitation.  
Voiceover: "OfficeFlow. Africa's Office Operating System. Making Operations Simpler."

## Nine module clips

### 1. HR Management — 35 seconds
- Hook: "Employee information should be organised, controlled and easy to review."
- Capture: authenticated HR workspace; test employee record; permitted detail view; role-denied view using a non-HR account.
- Voiceover: "OfficeFlow is building a structured home for employee information and HR processes, with access governed by role and organisation."
- Do not claim: compliant payroll, statutory calculations, full employee lifecycle, leave automation or confidential-field protection until each is tested.
- CTA: "See how OfficeFlow is structuring everyday office operations."

### 2. Finance & Administration — 35 seconds
- Hook: "Financial and administrative requests need clear ownership and review."
- Capture: test request; authorised review; approved/rejected status; audit history.
- Voiceover: "OfficeFlow is designed to bring operational finance and administration requests into a traceable process."
- Do not claim: a complete accounting ledger, bank reconciliation, tax compliance or payroll disbursement unless verified.
- CTA: "Bring more structure to office operations."

### 3. Tasks & Workflow — 45 seconds
- Hook: "A request should never disappear into an inbox."
- Capture: create request; route; approver action; status update; My Work; audit event. All steps must pass end-to-end first.
- Voiceover: "With OfficeFlow, a request can move through defined steps, with an owner, an approval path and a record of what happened."
- Do not record if create/edit, My Work reconciliation or approvals fail.
- CTA: "Request an OfficeFlow walkthrough."

### 4. Document Management — 35 seconds
- Hook: "A document record is only useful when the right person can securely retrieve the file."
- Capture: upload a harmless test PDF; confirm private storage; authorised download; denied download from another tenant; audit event.
- Voiceover: "OfficeFlow is working to connect business documents with the processes they support."
- Do not show metadata-only records as proof of secure file storage.
- CTA: "Keep work and its supporting records connected."

### 5. Email & Communication — 35 seconds
- Hook: "Work updates should stay connected to the work."
- Capture: in-app notification and its linked request. Show external email only after a real provider delivery and bounce/retry test.
- Voiceover: "OfficeFlow connects operational activity with in-app updates. External email delivery is enabled only when the organisation's provider is configured."
- Do not depict a sent email based only on a UI message.
- CTA: "Make operational communication easier to follow."

### 6. Calendar & Scheduling — roadmap clip only
- Hook: "Teams need a shared view of important dates."
- Visual: static product roadmap card, not a fabricated working calendar.
- Voiceover: "Calendar and scheduling are part of the OfficeFlow product direction. We are completing persistent event storage, permissions and reminder verification before presenting this as a live capability."
- CTA: "Follow the OfficeFlow product journey."

### 7. AI Productivity — roadmap clip only
- Hook: "AI should assist work without bypassing accountability."
- Visual: architecture / roadmap graphic; do not show a simulated assistant response as a live feature.
- Voiceover: "We are developing a governed AI layer that will respect existing permissions, provide sources for grounded answers and keep sensitive decisions under human control."
- Do not claim a live model integration until the provider response and security controls pass testing.
- CTA: "Follow what we're building."

### 8. Reports & Insights — 35 seconds
- Hook: "A dashboard is useful only when its numbers can be trusted."
- Capture: a defined metric; filters; source records; independent reconciliation; permission-limited view.
- Voiceover: "OfficeFlow's reporting direction is to make operational progress and bottlenecks easier to understand, with metrics tied to source records."
- Do not show unreconciled, hard-coded or cross-department data.
- CTA: "Make operational work more visible."

### 9. Security & Access — 35 seconds
- Hook: "Every organisation needs control over who can see and change its records."
- Capture: role matrix; authorised action; denied action; cross-tenant negative test; relevant audit record.
- Voiceover: "OfficeFlow is built around organisation-aware access and role-based permissions. We verify those controls through direct access tests."
- Do not claim certification, bank-grade security or complete compliance.
- CTA: "Build accountability into everyday operations."

## Recording and editing specification
- 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps; export H.264 MP4.
- Use a clean browser profile and synthetic data only.
- Hide browser bookmarks, personal email, tokens, IDs and unrelated tabs.
- Use restrained navy/brass title cards, readable captions and consistent closing frame.
- Keep narration calm and factual; no exaggerated claims or fake metrics.
- Add captions/subtitles and export a 9:16 crop for short-form social only when all key UI remains legible.
- Maintain a source log for every filmed capability: environment, test organisation, test date, tester, test steps and outcome.
