Workflow Bottlenecks

Poor Communication

Critical updates buried in WhatsApp groups. Context lost. Mistakes repeated.

Scattered Information

Files on USB, emails, Google Drive, WhatsApp — nobody knows where anything is.

Key Features

Everything Your Office Needs

One workflow platform for everyday operations.

Task & Workflow Management

Assign tasks, set deadlines, track progress. Full workflow visibility for every manager.

Approval System

Requests, approvals and rejections are recorded with an audit trail to reduce lost handoffs.

Centralized Communication

Task-based comments keep all context in one place. No more scattered WhatsApp messages.

Workflow Dashboard

The operations dashboard surfaces request and approval activity from saved workflow data.

Document Management

Upload, link, and organize documents by task or department. Find anything in seconds.

Notifications & Reminders

SLA deadlines and escalation rules help surface overdue approval steps. Outbound email delivery is not yet enabled.

People workflows

Configure employee-related requests and approvals with the workflow engine. Payroll calculations and statutory filing are not provided by this release.

Workflow Builder & Approvals

Configure approval steps, approver roles and service-level deadlines. AI-generated responses and actions are not enabled.

Workflow Operations Visibility

Review request counts, pending approvals and overdue steps from saved workflow records.
